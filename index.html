August 2015. A small site about the store, a few products listed, no shop and no online sales at all. Nothing before — so everything since is new.

Built the store and the theme, then worked out a repeatable recipe for product content and kept refining it. Mirror Mirror

mirrormirror.au

A directory built inside advertising law.

  • Dec 2025Launched
  • LeadDesign & build
  • CompliantBuilt inside strict advertising rules

In Australian cosmetic medicine, many of the words people search with cannot appear in public-facing copy. Type four letters of a restricted term and the search suggests the compliant wording instead.

WordPressPHPUI designPractitioner searchApproval workflowMaps
Read the full story

The problem

Strict advertising rules limit the brand names and treatment terms this directory can publish. People still need to find practitioners using the words they know.

From the fourth character of a restricted term, the search suggests the compliant wording. The results use that wording too: familiar input, compliant output.

Verified means verified

Practitioners apply through a long form and upload their documents. Their profile stays invisible. The owner reviews the documents and approves each application by hand; only then do the verified badge and public profile go live.

Finding someone

By name, treatment, keyword, distance from you, or visually on the map. Over 50 services.

My part

Lead developer and designer, from idea to launch. I led the design and build, coordinating the owner, a branding agency, the legal team and another developer. He wrote the term-matching code. The agency supplied the logo, colours, homepage direction and photos. Launched December 2025.
